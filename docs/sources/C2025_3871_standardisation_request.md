## Why this file replaces the C(2023) 3215 one

An earlier draft of Section 8 of the paper quoted Annex II point 2.3 of
Implementing Decision C(2023) 3215 of 22 May 2023. **That instrument has been
repealed.** Article 4 of C(2025) 3871 reads, in full:

> Implementing Decision C(2023)3215 is repealed.

C(2023) 3215 was drafted against the *proposal* for the AI Act, not the adopted
Regulation, which is visible in its own text: its Annex II cross-refers to
"Annex II, Section A of the Artificial Intelligence Act proposal". Recital (6)
of C(2025) 3871 gives the Commission's reason for replacing rather than amending
it, and records that CEN and Cenelec had reported significant delays.

Quoting a repealed instrument as if it were operative would have been a
first-order error in a paper whose argument is about what the law currently
requires. It is recorded here rather than quietly corrected.

## Annex I, item 3, verbatim

> Harmonised standard(s) and if necessary European standardisation deliverable(s) on record keeping through logging capabilities by AI systems

## Annex II, point 2.3, verbatim and in its entirety

> The harmonised standards and standardisation deliverables in this area shall set up specifications for record keeping. Those specifications shall comprehensively cover all elements referred to in Article 12 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

Thirty-two whitespace-separated words. It is a back-reference: it names no log
content of its own and instructs CEN and Cenelec to cover the elements of
Article 12, which is the provision Section 8 of the paper reads as bounded above
by three system-level purposes, with an enumerated minimum content that binds
Annex III point 1(a) alone.

The same formula runs through the 2025 annex. Points 2.1, 2.2, 2.7, 2.8 and 2.9
each say the deliverable "shall set up specifications for" the topic and that
those specifications "shall comprehensively cover all elements referred to in"
the corresponding Article. Under the 2025 request the length of point 2.3 is
therefore unremarkable, and the argument does not rest on its length. It rests
on the direction the reference points.

## Article 40(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, verbatim

Retrieved 2026-09-06 from the original Official Journal text,
`CELEX:32024R1689`, by extracting the document's own `art_40` element.

> High-risk AI systems or general-purpose AI models which are in conformity with harmonised standards or parts thereof the references of which have been published in the Official Journal of the European Union in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 shall be presumed to be in conformity with the requirements set out in Section 2 of this Chapter or, as applicable, with the obligations set out in of Chapter V, Sections 2 and 3, of this Regulation, to the extent that those standards cover those requirements or obligations.

(The "set out in of Chapter V" reads as it does in the published text.)

Article 41 provides the parallel route through Commission common specifications,
available only where the conditions in Article 41(1)(a) and (b) are met, among
them that no reference to a harmonised standard covering the requirement has
been published in the Official Journal and none is expected within a reasonable
period. Article 41(3) attaches the same presumption of conformity to a common
specification.
